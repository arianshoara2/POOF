Privacy Policy — POOF

Last updated: October 23, 2026
App: https://arianshoara2.github.io/POOF/

1. Who we are

POOF is a personal language-learning web app (German and English) developed by Arian Shoara. Contact: arian311380@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything you create in the app — flashcards, vocabulary progress, reading position, settings and preferences — is stored locally in your browser (localStorage / IndexedDB). The app works fully without signing in to any account.

3. Google Sign-In and Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, POOF uses your Google account only to identify you and to store a single backup file (poof-backup.json) in your Google Drive's private application data folder, using the drive.appdata scope. That folder is private to POOF: the app cannot see, read or modify any other file in your Drive. POOF does not access Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, Photos, or any other Google service, and it never reads your Drive files outside its own application data folder.

4. Dictionary and content lookups

Words you look up are sent to public dictionary services (Wiktionary, MyMemory) and, for transcripts, through the app's proxy to YouTube or podcast sources, solely to return the content you requested. No Google account data is attached to those requests.

5. What we never do

POOF does not sell personal data, does not show advertising, does not track you across sites, and does not share your personal data with any third party. The only copy of your backup outside your device is the backup file in your own Google Drive account, created only when you sign in and choose to sync.

6. Your control

You can sign out at any time from the Profile tab in the app. You can revoke POOF's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete locally stored data by clearing the site's data in your browser. Revoking access also stops any future backup; Google manages the application data folder itself.

7. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it. The Drive backup file stays in your Drive's application data folder until you revoke access or delete it through Google's app-data management.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: arian311380@gmail.com.

اگر با گوگل وارد شوید، POOF فقط برای شناسایی شما و ذخیره‌ی یک فایل بکاپ (poof-backup.json) در پوشه‌ی خصوصی داده‌های اپ در گوگل درایو شما از حسابتان استفاده می‌کند (مجوز drive.appdata). آن پوشه فقط مال POOF است: اپ هیچ فایل دیگری در درایو شما را نمی‌بیند و تغییر نمی‌دهد. ۵. چیزهایی که هرگز نمی‌کنیم

POOF داده‌ی شخصی نمی‌فروشد، تبلیغ نشان نمی‌دهد، شما را در سایت‌های دیگر دنبال نمی‌کند و داده‌ی شخصی‌تان را با هیچ شخص ثالثی شریک نمی‌شود. تنها نسخه‌ی بیرون از دستگاه شما، همان فایل بکاپ در درایو خودتان است که فقط وقتی وارد شوید و همگام‌سازی را انتخاب کنید ساخته می‌شود.
